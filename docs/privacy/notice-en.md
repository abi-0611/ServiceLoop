## [SHOP NAME] — how we handle your information

### What we collect

Your name and phone number, the vehicle you bring us, and the messages and
photographs exchanged about the work — including photographs our technicians
take of your vehicle, and recordings of calls where you were told at the start
of the call that it was being recorded.

We do not collect anything else, and we do not buy information about you from
anyone.

### Why we hold it

- To do the work you asked for.
- To tell you what is happening to your vehicle.
- To take your approval before spending your money.
- To invoice you.
- Where you have separately agreed to it, to remind you about work you deferred
  or a service that is due.

**Service updates and marketing are separate permissions.** Agreeing to be told
your car is ready is not agreeing to be sold anything, and refusing one does not
affect the other.

### How long we keep it

Conversations and photographs for as long as we are working with you, and for a
reasonable period afterwards in case you come back about the same job.

**Invoices and tax records for [8] years.** This is not our choice — the CGST
Act 2017 requires it. If you ask us to delete your data, these are kept with
your name and contact details removed.

### Your rights

At any time, and without giving a reason, you may:

- **Ask for a copy** of everything we hold about you. We send it as a file to
  your WhatsApp.
- **Ask us to correct** anything that is wrong.
- **Ask us to delete** your data.
- **Complain** to the person named below if you are not satisfied.

You can stop messages instantly by replying **STOP** on WhatsApp. That takes
effect immediately and permanently, and does not need anyone's approval.

Before we delete anything we check that the request really is from you. There is
a short period during which a deletion can be cancelled, because deletion cannot
be undone. Once it is done we send you a report of exactly what was deleted and
what had to be kept.
